00 / Before you start

Please read this first

Cobrá is a hackathon pilot on Celo mainnet, with real money. It is built so that nobody, including us, can hold or redirect your funds, but it is new and has not been audited yet.
  • Use small amounts only: a few reais, at most ~US$10. That is enough to test everything.
  • Never share your seed phrase or private key, with anyone, Cobrá included. Cobrá only asks for your public address (0x…).
  • Only pay through the link or QR the bot gives you. Never send tokens manually to a Cobrá contract: they would be stuck forever.
  • Double-check your receiving address at sign-up. Payments go straight to it and cannot be reversed.
  • Payments are capped at US$200 each and US$500 per day per merchant.

01 / What you need

Telegram and two wallets

TelegramOn your phone or computer.
Wallet 1 · merchantA Celo wallet that receives the money: MiniPay, Valora, or MetaMask with the Celo network. It needs no balance.
Wallet 2 · customerA different wallet (Cobrá refuses payments from the merchant's own address) holding a few USD₮ on the Celo network. About US$2 pays a R$10 invoice. No CELO needed for fees.

Getting USD₮ on Celo: buy it in MiniPay, or withdraw USD₮ from an exchange and choose the Celo network. Check the network twice: sending on the wrong network can lose the funds. USD₮ gets the best price in Brazil (~0.5–0.7% total cost, through Textile FX). USDC works in Cobrá too, but for wBRL it currently goes through a thin pool and may be refused.

02 / Merchant

Sign up (1 minute)

  1. Open t.me/cobra_agent_telegram_bot and tap Start.
  2. Choose Brasil (BRL). Switch the language with the Português / English buttons if you like.
  3. Paste the public address of Wallet 1 (starts with 0x). Check it character by character.
  4. The bot confirms "You'll receive wBRL at 0x…" and sends your fixed QR code: customers scan it and type the amount themselves.
  5. (Optional) Open the link the bot sends to prove the wallet is yours: one signature, no transaction.

03 / Merchant

Create a payment request

Send the bot an amount and a description, the way you write numbers in Brazil:

cobrar 10 teste

cobrar 10,50 café and cobrar 2 mil work too. The bot answers with a QR code, a payment link and a Share on WhatsApp button. The request is valid for 10 minutes; the bot reminds you 3 minutes before it expires.

04 / Customer · Wallet 2

Pay it

  1. Open the payment link on the phone where Wallet 2 is installed.
    • MiniPay: open it in MiniPay's browser, it connects by itself.
    • Otherwise tap Connect (or WalletConnect for a wallet on another device), then Switch to Celo if asked.
  2. Check the receipt on the page. The first quote can take a few seconds.
    Merchant receives10,00 wBRLdigital reais · 1 wBRL = R$1
    You pay≈ 1,85 USD₮digital dollars
  3. Tap Pay and sign in your wallet: one signature, not a transaction. No gas, nothing to approve.
  4. Wait a few seconds: Paying… then ✓ Paid, with a Celoscan link.

05 / Check

See the result

  1. Telegram: the bot sends "Pagamento recebido!" with the amount, the wBRL received and a Celoscan receipt link.
  2. Wallet 1 now holds exactly 10 wBRL.
  3. In the bot: /faturas (latest requests), /fechamento (today's summary + CSV, also sent automatically at 21:00), /painel (private web dashboard, link valid 15 minutes). Or just write: "o A-0001 foi pago?", "cancela a última cobrança".
  4. Every payment is public on cobra-beige.vercel.app/proof.

06 / Optional · not tested yet

Cash out to your bank

This goes through Ripio, not Cobrá. We have not tested it end to end yet, so your feedback here is very valuable.

  1. In the bot: /sacar. It sends a prefilled Ripio off-ramp link.
  2. Ripio asks you to sign up and verify your identity (CPF), and for a Pix key or bank account.
  3. Ripio shows a deposit address: send your wBRL there from your own wallet, on the Celo network.
  4. Before sending, check Ripio's minimum withdrawal: a few reais may be below it.

Cobrá never moves your funds: you send them yourself.

07 / Help

If something goes wrong

"Not enough balance" / "Pagar em reais"Wallet 2 does not hold enough USD₮ on Celo. Add some, then tap Try again.
"Switch to Celo"Your wallet is on another network: tap the button.
The request expiredAsk the bot for a new one (cobrar …). Nothing was charged.
Your wallet cannot signSome smart or delegated wallets are not supported yet. Try MiniPay, Valora or MetaMask. Nothing was charged.
The bot does not answerSend /start again, or tell us.

A failed payment never takes money: either the whole payment goes through, or nothing moves.
