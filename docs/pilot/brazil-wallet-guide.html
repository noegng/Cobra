Cobrá Wallet setup · Brazil · ~10 min

Set up a Celo wallet
and add funds.

For testers who have never used a crypto wallet. About 10 minutes, plus Ripio's identity check the first time. Then follow the pilot test guide.

Wallet choice still being confirmed. We recommend MiniPay; if it is not in your app store, use MetaMask (step 1b). Tell us which one worked for you.

00 / Three rules

Before you start

  1. Your recovery phrase (12 or 24 words) is the key to your money. Write it on paper, keep it offline, and never give it to anyone: not to Cobrá, not to "support", not to a website. Anyone who has it can take your funds.
  2. Always use the Celo network. Money sent on another network (Ethereum, Tron, BNB…) does not arrive and can be lost. Check the network every time you buy or withdraw.
  3. Start small: 10 to 20 reais is enough for the whole pilot.

You need two wallets for the full test (a merchant cannot pay their own invoice): one to receive (merchant) and one to pay (customer). Two phones, or two wallets in the same app, both work.

01 / Install

Install a wallet

1a · MiniPay (recommended, check availability)

  1. Install MiniPay from the official page (Play Store / App Store), or open it inside the Opera Mini browser.
  2. Create your account and follow the backup instructions carefully.
  3. MiniPay is built for Celo and stablecoins, and Cobrá's payment page connects to it automatically. When you send money to it from somewhere else, the network must still be Celo.

1b · MetaMask (if MiniPay is not available)

  1. Install MetaMask from metamask.io (official app only).
  2. Create a new wallet and write down the recovery phrase on paper.
  3. Add the Celo network: Networks → Add network → Add manually. Cobrá's payment page also offers Switch to Celo when you connect.

  4. (Optional) So the balances show, Import tokens on Celo:

    • USD₮ (digital dollar): 0x48065fbBE25f71C9282ddf5e1cD6D6A887483D5e, 6 decimals

02 / Address

Find your address

Your address starts with 0x and has 42 characters. It is public: you can share it to receive money.

  1. MiniPay: tap Receive / your account to copy it.
  2. MetaMask: tap the account name at the top to copy it.

Copy and paste it, never type it by hand. This is the address you give the Cobrá bot when you sign up as a merchant.

03 / Funds · customer wallet

Add funds: pick one path

Path A · in reais, with Pix (simplest)

You buy wBRL (1 wBRL = R$1) and pay the merchant directly in reais. No exchange rate, no conversion.

  1. Open Ripio's ramp, prefilled for R$20 of wBRL on Celo:
    ramp.ripio.com/?country=BR&chain=42220&token=WBRL&amount=20
  2. Paste the address of your customer wallet (step 02) when asked.
  3. Ripio asks you to sign up and verify your identity with your CPF (first time only).
  4. Pay with Pix. The wBRL arrive in your wallet, on Celo.
  5. The first time you pay a Cobrá invoice in wBRL, the page asks for a one-time "Authorize wBRL" step. If your wallet has no CELO for the network fee, Cobrá's agent sends you a little (0.05 CELO, automatically, once): just wait until the button is active. After that, every payment is a single signature.

We have not tested this Ripio purchase end to end yet: please tell us how long it took, the fees, and any problem. Cobrá's payment page also shows a "Pay in reais" button that opens this same Ripio link, prefilled with the invoice amount, when your balance is too low.

Path B · in digital dollars (USD₮)

For people who already save in dollars. You pay with USD₮; Cobrá converts and the merchant still receives reais (about 0.5–0.7% total cost).

  1. In MiniPay: use its Add cash / Deposit options, if available in Brazil.
  2. From an exchange: withdraw USD₮ (Tether) and, on the withdrawal screen, choose the Celo network. If Celo is not in the list, do not withdraw: that exchange cannot send to Celo.
  3. About US$2 pays a R$10 invoice. You do not need CELO for fees: Cobrá pays them.

Prefer USD₮ over USDC for Brazil: it gets the best price. USDC can be refused for wBRL payments for now.

04 / Check

Check, then start the pilot

  1. Your customer wallet shows wBRL (path A) or USD₮ (path B) on Celo.
  2. You can also paste your address on celoscan.io to see the balances.
  3. Now open the pilot test guide and sign up as a merchant with the other wallet.

05 / Help

Common problems

I sent funds but they do not showCheck you used the Celo network and the right address on celoscan.io. In MetaMask, import the token (step 1b).
The exchange does not offer CeloDo not withdraw from it. Use path A (Pix) or MiniPay.
"Authorize wBRL" stays greyed outCobrá's agent is sending you a little CELO for the fee: wait up to a minute.
Ripio refuses my verificationThat is on Ripio's side: try path B, and tell us what happened.
I lost my recovery phraseFunds cannot be recovered without it. That is why we test with small amounts only.

Never share your recovery phrase. Cobrá, Ripio and real support teams will never ask for it.
